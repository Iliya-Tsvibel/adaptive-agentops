# Stage 5.1 synthetic fixture reconciliation

Base `fd15fde5569df7a25e2bf848ff3b95605b681199`. Owner approved the
[Stage 5.0 proposal](https://github.com/Iliya-Tsvibel/adaptive-agentops/pull/27)
and its synthetic-only 5.1 slice in the campaign conversation.

Allowlist includes this contract, the campaign ledger,
`adaptive_agentops/offline_reconciliation.py`,
`tests/test_offline_reconciliation.py` and
`evaluations/stage5_offline_cases.json`. No runtime integration.

A case is a bounded synthetic projection for one requested dispatch.
Validate source coverage and bounded synthetic provenance before correlating
records. A complete source can attest to missing rows; partial coverage
cannot. An unavailable source has no rows. Invalid input takes precedence
over conflict, conflict over matching positive evidence, and positive
evidence over a bound no-send outcome and event. Otherwise an observed
reservation or claim yields UNRESOLVED, then source unavailability yields
UNAVAILABLE, then two complete empty observations yield MISSING_EVIDENCE.
Any remaining partial observation yields UNRESOLVED.

The Controller anchor represents corroborated reservation, action, approval
consumption, and session/run data. A complete source with a present but
uncorroborated anchor yields INCONSISTENT; partial input can leave it
unresolved. Any positive Target evidence needs the exact anchored digest.
A complete Controller observation with a missing anchor and a matching
Target claim signals an orphan and yields INCONSISTENT when the Controller
capture is at least as recent as the Target capture.

Freeze fixture expectations before implementation. Require exact results,
determinism, redacted output, immutable inputs, strict bounds and absence of
runtime, socket or database access. Native usage stays 0/4.

Independent review added explicit action and approval binding records to the
synthetic Controller projection. Manifest v2 retains the original expected
classifications and adds mismatched binding and missing pre-send digest
cases. A complete Controller capture at or after a Target claim must
contain the pre-send digest. A positive effect always requires a
corroborated historical approval consumption.

Manifest v3 adds earlier and later Target observations and freezes
representative provenance and reason codes. A later or equal-order Target
null receipt or complete missing claim contradicts a Controller effect
already recorded by that order. Earlier absence or null remains compatible
with later committed evidence.

Manifest v4 binds the outcome event's dispatch, action and session IDs.
An explicit unconsumed approval is contradictory under any source
coverage. Partial coverage can explain a missing approval row.

Manifest v5 requires the corroborating approval decision to be APPROVED
and includes a consumed rejection as a conflict.
